# 💫 About Me:
I'm Kulajit Banik, a software developer who enjoys building systems that stay fast and reliable at scale. My work spans distributed systems, cloud-native infrastructure, and AI-powered applications.<br><br>At RACCT, I built an IoT dashboard that handles telemetry for over 500,000 connected devices on a 99.9% uptime SLA. I also led development of a Claude API-powered voice assistant that handles 50,000+ interactions a day, and I cut its latency by 45% with prompt caching and smarter context handling. I've reduced database response times, brought release deployments down from 30 minutes to 3, and mentored junior engineers through stronger code review practices.<br><br>Before that, I took part in a two-year software engineering apprenticeship at Google, where I worked on high-availability systems and dug into consensus algorithms and fault tolerance. I also interned at Samsung Innovation Campus, working on embedded and mobile IoT software.<br><br>Outside of my day job, I build ambitious systems-level projects: a distributed in-memory data grid in C++, an eBPF-based service mesh, a machine-learned query optimizer, and a zero-trust Kubernetes security platform. I like working close to the metal as much as I like building the product on top of it.<br><br>I'm a B.Tech student in Computer Science and Engineering at Brainware University. I work in English and Hindi, and I'm learning German. I'm always interested in hard problems in infrastructure, AI, and performance engineering.<br><br>Software developer focused on distributed systems, cloud infrastructure, and LLM-powered applications. I've built platforms serving 500K+ devices and 50K+ daily AI interactions, and I like making systems faster, cheaper, and more reliable. Experience across RACCT, Google, and Samsung. Open to challenging problems in backend, infrastructure, and AI engineering.
